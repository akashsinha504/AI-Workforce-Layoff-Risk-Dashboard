# AI-Workforce-Layoff-Risk-Dashboard
Excel dashboard analyzing employee layoff risk using KPIs, slicers, and interactive charts.

## Project Overview
This Excel dashboard analyzes workforce layoff risk across industries, education levels, job roles, and AI adoption categories. The dashboard helps identify high-risk employee groups and supports data-driven workforce planning.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Slicers
- KPI Cards
- Data Visualization

dashboard-preview.png

## Key Features
- Total employee count and risk category KPIs
- Layoff risk distribution by High, Medium, and Low risk
- Industry-wise layoff risk analysis
- Job role-wise risk comparison
- AI adoption vs layoff risk analysis
- Education-wise workforce risk breakdown

## Key Insights
- 34% of employees are classified as High Risk.
- Higher AI adoption is linked with increased layoff risk.
- Healthcare, Telecom, and Finance show elevated risk.
- Support and operational roles are more vulnerable.

## Dataset
The dataset contains 20,000 employee records with details such as industry, education level, job role, AI adoption category, and layoff risk classification.



## Conclusion
This project demonstrates Excel dashboarding, workforce analytics, KPI reporting, and business insight generation.
